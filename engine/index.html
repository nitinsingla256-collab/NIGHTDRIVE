Car layer

A transparent PNG or WebP keeps its own background out of the scene. The built-in plates already contain a photographed car, so a new one is drawn on top of it — import a background too for a clean result. A cut-out is not attempted: an ordinary photo shows its full frame.

Car fit

A transparent PNG or WebP keeps its own background out of the scene. A normal photograph is placed as-is — it cannot be cut out automatically.
